# No-Waste Organics (NWO) — Master Index

## 1. Overview

NWO is a **multi-disciplinary framework for Industrial Ecology, Advanced Energy, and Synthetic Biology**. It represents a blueprint for a fully circular, highly advanced metabolic system.

**Core Principle:** Waste from one process becomes input for another. Energy is captured and recycled. Biological and physical systems are integrated.
