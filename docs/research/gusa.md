# GUSA Research Foundation

## Purpose

This document records the GUSA research framing as an exploratory research area.

It does not describe implemented production functionality.

## Research Boundary

GUSA research may inform future architecture discussions, but it does not become production architecture without specification, validation, implementation, testing, and review.

## Research Objectives

- Define candidate system models.
- Separate conceptual claims from implemented behavior.
- Identify future validation requirements.
- Preserve clear separation between research documents and production code.

## Production Relationship

Production documentation describes implemented or approved architecture.

This document describes research objectives only.

No statement in this file should be interpreted as evidence that a feature exists in the production codebase.

## Future Work

Future updates may add formal definitions, diagrams, validation criteria, and links to supporting research notes.
