# Indra's Net Research Foundation

## 1. Purpose

This document records Indra's Net as an exploratory research area for distributed sensing, mesh coordination, environmental telemetry, and defensive network resilience.

It does not describe implemented production functionality within the active software crates.

## 2. Research Boundary

Indra's Net research is intended to explore candidate distributed network models, telemetry coordination patterns, defensive infrastructure concepts, and future validation requirements.

It remains separate from production architecture until formally specified, implemented, tested, and reviewed.

## 3. Long-Term Research Objectives

Future investigations may evaluate:

- **Distributed Sensing Meshes:** Candidate models for decentralized telemetry collection and coordination.
- **Environmental Signal Mapping:** Possible methods for representing environmental observations across spatial and temporal layers.
- **Resilient Peer Coordination:** Fault-tolerant node communication, local autonomy, and fail-closed behavior.
- **Joint Communication and Sensing:** Exploratory study of systems that combine communication paths with sensing functions.
- **Indra's Shield:** Defensive research into privacy-preserving, resilient, and tamper-aware infrastructure protection for distributed nodes.
- **Governed Data Promotion:** Candidate rules for moving raw telemetry into validated, cryptographically sealed records.

## 4. Production Relationship

Production documentation describes implemented or approved software architecture.

This document describes research objectives only.

No statement in this file should be interpreted as evidence that Indra's Net or Indra's Shield exists as an implemented production network, telemetry mesh, or active runtime feature.
