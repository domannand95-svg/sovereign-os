# Sovereign Stack Research Foundation

## 1. Purpose

This document records Sovereign Stack as an exploratory research area for layered system models, governed autonomy, and future cross-domain integration studies.

It does not describe implemented production functionality within the active software crates.

## 2. Research Boundary

Sovereign Stack research is intended to explore candidate layered architectures, validation pathways, governance interfaces, and future integration constraints.

It remains separate from production architecture until formally specified, implemented, tested, and reviewed.

## 3. Long-Term Research Objectives

Future investigations may evaluate:

- **Layered System Models:** Candidate ways to organize software, governance, telemetry, memory, distributed coordination, and research interfaces.
- **Governed Autonomy:** Mechanisms for keeping autonomous optimization systems advisory until validated and explicitly approved.
- **Cross-Domain Integration:** Research pathways for connecting software architecture with ecology, sensing, formal methods, and future hardware concepts.
- **Bioregional Systems:** Candidate models for ecological monitoring, native apiculture support, botanical systems, and environmental telemetry.
- **Kinetic and Plasma Telemetry:** Exploratory models for future simulation, monitoring, and validation of high-energy physical systems.
- **Jurisdictional Sovereignty Nodes:** Research into governance, legal, geographic, and mathematical models for preserving informational autonomy across independent operating contexts.
- **Validation Pipelines:** Candidate rules for moving ideas from research notes into reviewed architecture specifications.
- **Capability Boundaries:** Mechanisms for preventing research concepts from silently entering production behavior.

## 4. Production Relationship

Production documentation describes implemented or approved software architecture.

This document describes research objectives only.

No statement in this file should be interpreted as evidence that Sovereign Stack exists as an implemented production platform, deployed legal structure, ecological system, plasma system, or active runtime feature.
